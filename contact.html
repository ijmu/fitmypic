Reporting a problem with a tool

Requests to fetch a remote image, a resize that produces an unexpected size, or an export that will not download are all diagnosable from a description. Include:

  • The page URL and which preset or tool you were using.
  • Browser and version, plus device type — phone or desktop matters for memory limits.
  • Source format (JPG, PNG, or WebP) and approximate dimensions and file size.
  • The output settings you chose: target dimensions, output format, quality, and any KB target.
  • What appeared on screen: the measured dimensions or file size reported, and any error wording verbatim.

When a tool seems to do nothing at all, try a hard reload and a second image first. It is often a stale cached script rather than the tool, and that is worth ruling out before emailing.

Do not send us your photos

Please do not attach images unless a specific file is needed to reproduce a defect. FitMyPic processes files locally in your browser, so we have no server copy to inspect, and you would be sharing a file for no diagnostic benefit. If a repro image is genuinely required, send a sample you are comfortable disclosing — never an identity document, a scanned contract, or a photo of another person.

Preset and size corrections

Presets such as the square 1024 × 1024, the Instagram set, and the passport-photo guidance follow published requirements at the time of writing. Platforms and issuing authorities change their specifications. If one of our figures is out of date, send the page URL and the current requirement as documented by the platform or authority, and the guide will be corrected.

Privacy requests

There are no accounts and no stored images, so most access or deletion requests have nothing to act on. If your request concerns Google Analytics data or a message you sent us, describe what you need and the relevant time period. See the privacy policy for what is collected and why.

Advertising and partnerships

Advertising on FitMyPic is served through Google AdSense. We do not sell placement inside tool results, and we do not accept payment to alter an output measurement. Partnership enquiries are welcome at the address above.

When to expect a reply

Messages are answered as time allows, generally within a few days. Reports containing the detail listed above are resolved fastest. If you are working to a deadline — a submission portal, a print run — please keep your original files and verify exported dimensions before you rely on them.
