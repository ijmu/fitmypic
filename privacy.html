Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 8 October 2026

How your photos are handled

FitMyPic resizes, crops, compresses, converts, and removes backgrounds from images. All of that work happens in your browser using the Canvas API and WebAssembly. When you select a photo, the browser reads it from your device, the tool transforms the pixels in memory, and you receive the result as a download. No FitMyPic server receives your photo.

You do not have to take that on trust. Open your browser's developer tools, switch to the network panel, and resize a photo. You will see page assets loading and no request carrying image data. There is no upload field and no endpoint that accepts one.

Particularly sensitive images

People use image tools for identity photographs, scanned documents, and product shots. Because processing is local, those files stay with you. Two caveats are honest to state anyway:

  • On a shared or public computer, the download you save and the browser's local cache remain on that machine. Clear site data and your downloads folder when you are finished.
  • Anything you later post to a social platform leaves your device at that point, under that platform's terms. FitMyPic has no part in that step.

What FitMyPic does not collect

  • Image pixels — never transmitted, in any form.
  • Photo metadata — EXIF data present in your file is not read, logged, or transmitted.
  • Filename, dimensions, or output size — shown to you on screen to confirm the result, never sent anywhere.
  • Account details — FitMyPic has no registration, login, or saved profiles.

Local storage and offline caching

FitMyPic keeps your light or dark theme preference in localStorage. It holds no identifier and cannot be read by other websites. A service worker caches the site's own pages and assets so the tools work on a weak connection and load quickly on return visits; it never caches the images you open. Clearing site data in your browser removes both.

Advertising

Ads on this site are served through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to select ads, measure their performance, cap frequency, and detect invalid activity.

Google's advertising cookies allow it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites. To opt out of personalised advertising, visit Google Ads Settings. For a fuller account of how Google handles data from partner sites, see Google's advertising technologies page. Other ad vendors may also set cookies under their own policies.

Blocking or deleting advertising cookies does not disable any FitMyPic feature. The image tools work identically whether or not ad cookies are present.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-XDE7CN1DH1) to see which tools and guides people actually use, in aggregate — pages viewed, approximate region, and device type. That information guides what we build next; it is not used to identify anyone, and it is not combined with other data. Google Analytics sets cookies and may process technical data such as a truncated IP address under Google's privacy policy. The Google Analytics opt-out add-on disables it.

Web hosting

The site is delivered as static files by a third-party host. That host may keep standard access logs — IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp — for security and operational purposes. This is ordinary infrastructure logging outside our control and subject to the provider's policy.

Retention and your choices

Since photos and their details never reach us, there is no image data to retain or erase. Aggregate analytics is kept for the standard Analytics retention period. You may ask what data relates to your use of this site, or ask for it to be deleted, by writing to the address below.

Children

FitMyPic is a general image utility, not a service aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information via the contact address, tell us and we will remove it.
