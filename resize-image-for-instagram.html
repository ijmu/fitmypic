What this Instagram preset does

The tool creates a real 1080 × 1080 image rather than only changing the preview. Crop to fill uses the whole square without distorting the subject. Fit entire image keeps every edge and adds canvas space when the source is portrait or landscape.

Crop or fit: which should you choose?

Crop works well for centered portraits, product photos, and artwork with clear space around the subject. Choose fit when text, logos, prices, or product edges must remain visible. Set the canvas color before exporting a fitted JPG.

Check the image before posting

Compare Original and Result to inspect framing and fine text. A square post can also appear as a smaller profile-grid tile, so avoid placing essential details directly against an edge. The measured output line confirms dimensions, format, and encoded file size.

Instagram image questions

Is 1080 × 1080 still useful for Instagram?

Yes. It remains a practical square format for feed graphics, product posts, announcements, and images designed to read consistently in a profile grid.

Will this tool stretch my photo?

No. Crop trims the longer dimension and fit adds canvas space. Both preserve the source proportions.

Should I use JPG or PNG?

JPG is usually smaller for photography. PNG is useful for graphics, screenshots, sharp text, or transparency. WebP is available when the destination accepts it.
