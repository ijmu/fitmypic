# FitMyPic Product Truth

FitMyPic is a browser-local image utility for people who need to resize, crop, compress, compare, and export everyday JPG, PNG, or WebP files without an account.

The primary success action is simple: choose an image, make a small set of precise adjustments, inspect the measured result, and download it. Existing browser processing, SEO content, privacy wording, legal pages, and accessible native controls are product requirements.
