    FAQ

    Before you edit

    Are my images uploaded anywhere?

    No. Background removal, resizing, compression, and previews run locally in your browser. Your files are not sent to a FitMyPic server.

    What kinds of backgrounds can be removed?

    Solid and near-solid backgrounds work best. Detailed or busy scenes may need a dedicated AI editor.

    Can I hit an exact file-size limit?

    For JPG and WebP, FitMyPic searches for the closest measured result. Exact bytes cannot be guaranteed because image content affects compression.

    Which format should I export?

    Use PNG for transparency and sharp graphics, JPG for broadly compatible photos, or WebP for modern web delivery. The measured output size is shown before download.
